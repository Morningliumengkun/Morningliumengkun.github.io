关于我About Me

我是张三，示例大学计算机科学与技术学院 2023 级博士研究生，导师为李四教授。此前于 2023 年在示例理工大学获得计算机技术硕士学位，于 2020 年在示例大学获得计算机科学与技术学士学位。我的研究兴趣包括机器学习、计算机视觉与多模态学习，目前致力于面向边缘设备的高效多模态感知研究。 I am a Ph.D. student (2023–present) at the School of Computer Science, Example University, advised by Prof. Si Li. I received my M.S. in Computer Technology from Example Tech University in 2023 and my B.S. in Computer Science from Example University in 2020. My research interests lie in machine learning, computer vision, and multimodal learning, currently focusing on efficient multimodal perception for edge devices.

攻读博士学位期间，我以第一作者身份在 CVPR、AAAI 等国际会议发表论文多篇，曾获研究生国家奖学金、校一等奖学金等奖励。我热爱开源社区，欢迎通过邮件与我交流合作。 During my Ph.D. study, I have published several first-author papers at international venues such as CVPR and AAAI, and received honors including the National Graduate Scholarship. I am an open-source enthusiast and always happy to collaborate — feel free to reach out by email.
